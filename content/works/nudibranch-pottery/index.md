## Inspiration

My late wife (a graduate of SVA in NYC, who passed away at a young age from lung adenocarcinoma) practiced pottery painting. I also once made several ceramic figures by shaping pottery clay.

Sea slugs.

I made these around the time I came across a book of nudibranch photographs and was fascinated by their beauty.

![Sea Slug 01](nudibranch-01.jpg)

![Sea Slug 02](nudibranch-02.jpg)

## What Happened Afterwards

They were displayed at my wife's pottery exhibitions and other shows, but before I knew it, they had all disappeared.

Maybe they're still with the people who bought them.
